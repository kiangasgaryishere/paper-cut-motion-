# Connected Speech — original treatment before demo study

Supplied script: script.md. Voice: uploaded mommy.mp3. Style: Marker on Paper, selected by the user. Audience: Persian-speaking English learners; provisional A2–B1. Format: Instagram portrait. Effects only, no music, retaining the user's established preference. Captions Persian; target-English examples separate.

## User decisions

- Start with a marker reconstruction of the film situation; the user may supply the actual clip later if needed.
- Produce the complete film after reviewing the plan.
- User approves art direction/storyboard/style frames, then production proceeds independently.

## Candidate visual structures (same supplied script)

1. A listening puzzle followed by a four-step worked lesson: film situation, subtitles-on/off contrast, sound-link transformation, repeatable practice. Best match for one-question-per-sheet and a concrete learner takeaway.
2. A continuous branching mind map with character reactions. Rejected: conflicts with distinct paper sheets and risks a crowded knowledge map.
3. A sequence of English word cards and generic cinema inserts. Rejected: too typographic and weak at showing why knowing words differs from hearing linked speech.

## Selected treatment

Use original option 1. The paper is always the frame. Reconstruct a tiny adventure-film interaction in economical black marker geometry, not a photoreal scene or a purported original movie clip. The exact source dialogue/audio must be established from the supplied recording or explicitly resolved; do not invent a movie quotation or fake actor audio.

Then compare readable individual words (blue givens) against what the ear needs to identify (one red focus). Build the did + you -> didja example piece by piece. Emphasize that it can occur in fast/natural speech, not that it is obligatory for every speaker. Finish with a three-pass listening loop and the supplied LISTEN comment CTA. Plan a meaningful drawing/highlight/camera/page event every 3–4 seconds, while keeping the example readable.

Black builds structure; blue marks known written forms; red is the final answer/target stroke per sheet. Every page has one question. No glossy whiteboard, hand/pen graphic, yellow fill, gradient/shadow decoration, or busy transition pack. Top-down camera only, native push-through/peel transitions. Paper-slip Persian captions are fixed in screen space, separate from examples. Character identity is kept consistent.

## Review before production

Analyze source voice and style reference first. Prepare timed storyboard, sound plan and three original style frames (hook, did-you explanation, practice/CTA). No full film animation or final mix until the user has reviewed the plan.
