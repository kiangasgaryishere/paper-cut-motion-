# Art direction v1 — ready for user review

Primary style: Marker on Paper, Lemo-Opuscar `3a7132b996be0241c1ea0ab180d519f06e6f10b6`. See STYLE_ANALYSIS.fa.md for the detailed Persian analysis and reference URLs.

- Warm matte paper #FAF9F5 fills the 1080×1920 frame. No desk, page edge, hand, visible marker, gradient, vignette, glow or decorative shadow.
- Bullet marker: 6–10 px near-constant width, mild deterministic wobble, dense touch-down/corners, lift at end, ink-density crossing behavior. Three boil drawings on twos; typography stable.
- Black structure; small blue given-information marks; one final red focus per page. Native pen/density code is used, with portrait dimensions and local helper import adapted.
- English Kalam Bold/Regular, actually revealed through glyph-centerline masks in production. Persian Noto Sans Arabic on the fixed torn-paper caption slip; preserve joined Persian shaping.
- Top-left short English question/instruction, circled page number upper-right. Main drawings between roughly y=550 and 1450; caption slip below. No important text partially outside frame.
- Top-down camera, native follow/lean-in/pull-back only; maximum lean-in 2×, chosen from real text bounds. Redraw vectors through camera, not scaled screenshots.
- Peel and push-through only. No transition collection from the previous film.
- Film reconstruction uses original simple black-line adventure characters; no actor likeness or original film video is asserted. The existing source English audio is retained.
- Educational emphasis: first hear without the answer, then read; distinguish known written forms from the heard connected pronunciation; one idea per page.
- No music. Felt-marker/paper sounds with clear speech priority, especially during English pronunciation.
- Native motion cadence: 24 fps output, 12 drawing updates/s, smooth camera. This chosen style-specific cadence overrides the generic 30 fps default.

Three real style frames are in review/. The prototype is a gallery, not the full film. Original treatment was written before reading demo story/code. No LemoLab demo end-card/branding or plot is copied.
