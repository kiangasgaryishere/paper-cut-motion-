# Original treatment — written before demo story study

Supplied script and voice promote five actual user-stated Rahnama service features, with an open loop on the fifth and a single comment CTA «برنامه». No claims, staff counts, prices, guarantees or testimonials are added.

Three internal structures considered:

1. A continuously travelled five-station roadmap, where the fifth station feeds changes back into the first. Chosen: the spatial argument expresses personalization, continuous support and iterative planning, while fulfilling the selected one-board style.
2. A left/right generic-class vs Rahnama comparison. Rejected: too repetitive and risks making unsubstantiated competitor claims beyond the script.
3. Five separate poster panels. Rejected: breaks the selected native continuous-whiteboard camera grammar and resembles the prior paper-page films.

Chosen direction: one glossy board; live black handwriting; blue routes/process and coral feedback/important objects. Hook: an already-started large Persian handwritten «ثبت‌نام نکن!» with an enrollment-book symbol and five visible checkpoints; number five receives the coral unknown ring as “especially the last” is spoken. Each station resolves one feature with small relevant icons and actual scheduling windows, not a list of claims typed on slides. The last station's logged work→team review→next week loop is the payoff. A brief full-board view reveals the system, followed by a continuous push into the comment keyword «برنامه».

No music. Supplied narration retained complete and unchanged in speed. Native marker-on-gloss, nib ticks, a restrained eraser rub and magnet clicks are speech-ducked and tied to actual events. Format is 1080×1920, 24fps, approximately 76 seconds including a final hold. Original figures are conceptual adult learner/team diagrams, not staff portraits or student evidence.

Brand adaptation: the explicitly selected Whiteboard style takes precedence over the general Swiss/cut-paper/oil-texture templates. Retain Rahnama blue #124CB5, coral #F15B45, charcoal #10171B; no emojis or invented logo. Board wording uses the style's native single-line lettering. Morabba binary assets cannot be read through the cloud skill or found on this executor; captions use the available joined Noto Sans Arabic, clearly identified as a fallback, not Morabba. Proprietary font files will not be publicly redistributed. A future authorized font substitution can change localization without redesigning the board.
