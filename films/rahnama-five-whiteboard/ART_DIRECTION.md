# Art direction

Official Lemo Opuscar Whiteboard: one glossy dry-erase board, EMS Tech single-line strokes, floating physical markers without hands, one eraser and two restrained selection/revision pins. No paper pages, cut-paper collage or painted texture. One continuous world/camera path, no hard cuts. Three temporal subframes on fast travel soften camera movement.

Localized Persian warning, school mention and CTA use centerline paths from joined Noto Sans Arabic; dot components are retained separately so Persian letters remain identifiable. Other labels use native EMS Tech. Dark text #10171B; route/process blue #124CB5; emphasis and review coral #F15B45. Red is reserved for meaningful highlights, not arbitrary coloured text.

Raised Persian captions: 43px Noto Sans Arabic, light translucent label with dark lettering and blue edge; bottom270px, left94px/right94px. Max two lines, never overlapping reading diagrams. Grouped board labels are hidden whole if their bounds would cross reading safe space during travel. At the full-board overview, tiny labels serve context only.

Typography limitation: authorized Morabba binary was not accessible in this executor; do not claim its use. Existing licensed Noto font is reused from prior film, no proprietary font copied. No official logo was supplied; plain “رهنما” is an ordinary name mention, not a recreated logo.
