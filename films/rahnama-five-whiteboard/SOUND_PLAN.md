# Sound design

Supplied narration is the single time authority. Preserve all decoded 3,604,000 samples at48k, pad only the end to76s. No music, score, voice regeneration or scene-level narration gain resets.

Native Opuscar Whiteboard felt-on-gloss sound: each visible stroke has band-passed body/hiss, short landing tick and occasional restrained squeak; colour timbres subtly vary. SFX pan follows camera-relative position. Off-camera strokes are attenuated. The visible wipe has felt friction; actual selection/revision pins have steel-face clicks/low board resonance. A soft air pass only accompanies continuous camera travel. No unrelated transition booms or comic effects.

Master narration across the complete recording; sidechain effects from voice envelope, no effect louder than speech. Final stereo target near−14LUFS, true peak≤−1dBTP. Retain effects/voice/mix stems locally for audit and future revisions. No unnecessary external SFX Kit assets: the original native whiteboard synthesis matches the material more precisely and adds no asset licensing ambiguity.

QA reports measurements and source alignment, not a human listening review if none was performed.
