## Camera / humour

Overhead slow travel across a coherent album, push into one photo with exact crop match, let the photograph's scene move, then return. Lateral background smear is directional and limited to moving scene; subject remains clearer. Small parallax in live photos is supported by separately composed layers if available. Do not claim real character/body animation where artwork is only a still.

Different staging: memorial portrait, UFO panorama, two-card identity change, sports trophy photo, football journey photostrip, conclave photo, recursive award Polaroid, hosts' retirement photograph. Only selected beats get a bounce, comic crash/scale gag or flash; no overlapping piles of animated text, whips and loud effects. Simplicity remains the baseline.
